He who doesn't fear death dies only once. - Giovanni Falcone

I am a Theoretical Physicist, currently pursuing a PhD at the College of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in University of Houston.

Emergent collective behaviour in many-body systems is at the heart of my research. In my past work, I showed how quantum correlations can be generated and protected in open quantum systems initialized out of equilibrium. We conjecture that collective behaviour, such as synchronisation, provides a mechanism for this protection and a signature of underlying quantum correlations at mesoscopic and macroscopic scales. I also showed how integrability emerges in a time-dependent multistate Landau–Zener model and in the two-dimensional helical SYK model. More recently, my goal has been to determine how recurrences (periodic orbits), integrability, chaos, and driving shape thermalization and quantum memory, and to clarify the scope of the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis (ETH). Ultimately, I am interested in understanding the structure of spacetime and gravity at quantum scales, using all the tools I have acquired over the years working on different problems.

Research Interests

  • Integrability and Chaos in Many-body quantum systems
  • Open Quantum Systems
  • Quantum Field Theory
  • Quantum Phase Transitions
  • Quantum Gravity
  • Dualities in physics - underlying connections in seemingly unrelated physical systems
